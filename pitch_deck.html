Narrative

The category gap: spectators can bet, players can grind — but they rarely share one real-time economy.

What’s broken today

  • Most betting products are detached from gameplay and feel passive.
  • Traditional on-chain settlement is slow for short combat rounds.
  • Player skill often has weak economic upside in-session.
  • Spectators have no high-frequency, event-driven participation.

Kartfall thesis

  • Skill drives outcomes, not opaque odds engines.
  • Settlement follows gameplay tempo, not chain confirmation tempo.
  • Watchers become participants through in-match markets.
  • Wallet UX stays simple with Privy + profile redemption flow.

We are building the skill-to-earn spectator arena for fast-session multiplayer.

Economics

Monetization is embedded in gameplay velocity, not ads.

Staked Match Economics

  • Platform rake: 4% of stake pool.
  • Distributable pool allocated by in-match K/D performance.
  • Supports variable stake sizes by room options.
stake_revenue = 0.04 × total_stake_pool

Prediction Market Economics

  • Platform rake: 2.5% of market pool.
  • Player incentive cut: 1% to the bet-on player.
  • Current ticket size boundaries: 0.1 to 10 USDC.
bet_revenue = 0.025 × total_bet_volume
